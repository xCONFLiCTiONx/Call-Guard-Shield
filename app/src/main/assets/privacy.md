# Call Guard Shield Privacy Policy
**Effective Date: August 4, 2026**

Call Guard Shield is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application handles your information when you use our Android application for call filtering, spam detection, and local database management.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Local Call and Device Data
*   **Call State and Logs**: The app accesses incoming call states and local call logs solely to compare numbers against your local blocklists and provide real-time filtering functionality.
*   **Contacts Data**: The app accesses your device's contacts solely to identify "Verified Contacts" and allow them to bypass the firewall. Your contact information is processed locally on your device and is **not** uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party, including Gemini AI.
*   **Local Database**: Any blacklists, whitelists, or custom rules you configure are stored locally on your device.

### Gemini Intelligence Lookups
If you utilize optional features powered by Gemini intelligence for phone number analysis, relevant query data (phone number only) necessary to perform the lookup is processed securely by Google's Gemini API.

### Cloud Backup (Google Drive)
If you enable Google Account Sync, the app will access your Google email address and use a private folder in your Google Drive to store encrypted backups of your settings and history. We do not have access to any other files in your Google Drive.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The information processed by Call Guard Shield is used strictly for the following purposes:
*   Executing local call blocking and spam filtering rules.
*   Managing your personal blacklists and whitelists directly on your device.
*   Providing intelligent phone number insights when requested.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
*   **No Third-Party Sale**: We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or call logs to third parties.
*   **Service Providers**: Data is only shared with trusted API services (such as Gemini intelligence endpoints) strictly to deliver the specific features you trigger within the app.

## 4. Data Security
We implement appropriate technical measures to ensure that your local database and call handling rules remain secure on your device. Communication with external intelligence APIs is encrypted in transit.

## 5. Data Retention
Local intelligence reports and caller identification results are cached on your device for up to 30 days to improve performance. You can manually wipe this cache at any time in the app settings.

## 6. User Controls and Data Deletion
You maintain full control over your data:
*   You can clear your local blocklists, logs, and custom rules at any time directly through the app settings or by uninstalling the application.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date.

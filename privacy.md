# Call Guard Shield Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 4, 2026

Call Guard Shield is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application handles your information when you use our Android application for call filtering, spam detection, and local database management.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Local Call and Device Data
- **Call State and Logs:** The app accesses incoming call states and local call logs solely to compare numbers against your local blocklists and provide real-time filtering functionality.
- **Contacts:** If enabled, the app accesses your contacts (`READ_CONTACTS`) to ensure known people are never blocked and to allow for "Contacts Only" filtering modes.
- **Location Data:** The app may request access to your location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) to determine your current state. This is used solely to power the "Out of State" blocking feature, which blocks calls from area codes outside your current region.

### AI-Powered Investigation (Gemini)
If you utilize the optional "AI Investigation" or "Deep Scan" features for phone number analysis:
- **Number Analysis:** The phone number is sent to Google's Gemini API. 
- **Web Research:** The AI may perform real-time web research to identify the caller's identity, reputation, and potential spam/scam risks.
- **User-Provided API Key:** You must provide your own Gemini API key, which is stored securely on your device.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- Executing local call blocking and spam filtering rules.
- Managing your personal blacklists and whitelists directly on your device.
- Providing intelligent phone number insights via AI when requested.
- Comparing your current location (state) against caller area codes for regional filtering.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
- **No Third-Party Sale:** We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or call logs to third parties.
- **Service Providers:** Data is only shared with trusted API services (such as Gemini intelligence endpoints) strictly to deliver specific features triggered within the app.

## 4. Data Security
We implement appropriate technical measures to ensure that your local database and call handling rules remain secure on your device.

## 5. User Controls
You can clear your local blocklists, logs, and custom rules at any time directly through the app settings or by uninstalling the application.
